# 0002. Maintain Dual Artifacts: JAMS for Evidence and Music-IR for Consumption

We decided to produce both `analysis.jams` and `music-ir.json` per track. JAMS acts as an immutable, standards-compliant archive of multi-candidate timing annotations and tool provenance, while `music-ir.json` serves as the concise domain representation optimized for multimodal agent reasoning and sound synthesis parameterization.
